# Inactive E3 extraction outer source

`inactive_extraction_outer.bind_and_run` is an adapter, not an admitted launcher.
It has no CLI, fixed ROOT decision digest, held source/heavy lock, live resource
admission, or one-shot authority. Do not invoke it until a separately reviewed
ROOT wrapper owns those conditions and the exact H source and F loader results
are sealed. The original failed F and E3 evidence remains unchanged.

The accepted F-loader H lifecycle source at `83824ecd` provides the sole
Docker create/CID recovery, owned wait, stream/session closure, cleanup and
quarantine implementation. Its three imported files and source/ROOT/independent
seals are authenticated before binding. The isolated process substitutes only
four named predicates: E3 source/material admission, selected guest Cmd,
authentic inspect Cmd with the unchanged six mounts and security profile, and
nonpublishing guest phase receipts. `bind_and_run` restores H globals afterward.
The future wrapper must stage reviewed H bytes under `/bundle/h-source` and
retain that root and the decision unchanged through physical closure.

The selected bundle holds an exact canonical `e3-material-pins.json` with all
nine recipe inputs, E3 guest and recipe source bytes, accepted tool/runtime
tables, external 477-byte lock, and H source/review seals. The guest performs
the reviewed E3 source/F-copy preparation, then runs normal followed by
sanitizer inventory extraction into `/out/emissions-{normal,sanitize}`. H's
owned capture retains each raw argv/stream/numeric wait in `/out/phases` and
the extraction-only result in `/out/evidence`. A failed or uncertain phase
preserves its partial stage and does not publish a fixture object.

Still required: actual accepted copied-F loader manifest, compatibility and
reviews; full tracked E3 source admission; a current source/heavy lease and
host/image/tool admission; immutable bundle and decision; ROOT one-shot wrapper;
and separate review of emitted inventories. This source does not establish
native fixture, N3, sanitizer, or Section 9 acceptance.
